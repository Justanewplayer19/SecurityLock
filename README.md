# 🔒 SecurityLock Plugin

SecurityLock is a privacy-focused plugin for the [Vendetta](https://github.com/vendetta-mod/Vendetta) and Revenge Discord clients.  
It allows you to lock Discord behind a passcode or password, so nobody can snoop on your DMs, servers, or account without permission.

## Features
- Lock Discord with either a **passcode** or **password**
- Works on:
  - Direct Messages (DMs)
  - Selected Servers
- **Forgot option**: recover with your alternate password/passcode
- Protects against family, friends, or parents who might peek into your Discord
- Seamless lock screen when launching Discord

## 📥 Installation

1. Open the **Plugins** page inside Vendetta or Revenge.
3. Reload Discord. SecurityLock will now be active.

## About

Worried about someone checking your device? SecurityLock has you covered.  
Whether you want to keep your chats private from family, relatives, or strict parents, this plugin ensures Discord stays locked down until **you** unlock it.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
This plugin is for **personal privacy** only.  
It does not encrypt Discord data. It simply adds a lock layer to prevent casual snooping.
